Entry-Level Data Jobs Compared

A non-commercial student research project that compares entry-level job markets for data and analytics roles across 18 countries, to help with the choice of where to study abroad.

What it studies

Roles: Data Analyst, Business Analyst, other analytics roles, Data Scientist, Data Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer and AI Engineer.

RegionCountries
North AmericaUSA, Canada
OceaniaAustralia, New Zealand
EuropeIreland, Netherlands, Germany, Sweden, Italy, France, Denmark, Norway, Finland
Asia and Middle EastHong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, UAE (Dubai)

Questions it answers

  • How many entry-level roles does each country have, relative to its population?
  • What share of jobs are entry-level, and how many "junior" roles still ask for 3+ years?
  • What is entry-level pay after adjusting for purchasing power?
  • How often is a local language required?
  • How often do postings mention visa sponsorship?
  • Which skills are in demand, and in which cities?

How the data is used

Job postings are gathered from public job-board interfaces and partner APIs, used only for aggregate statistics, and not republished. The project does not sell data, run advertising or charge for access. Any figures shown are counts, shares and medians per country, never individual listings.
